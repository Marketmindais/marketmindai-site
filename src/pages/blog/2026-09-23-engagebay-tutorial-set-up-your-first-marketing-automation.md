## What is marketing automation and why you need it now

Marketing automation just means you set up rules for your marketing tasks. It means your emails, messages, and follow-ups happen on their own. You don't have to manually send a welcome email every time someone signs up. The system does it.

This frees up your time. It also makes sure no one falls through the cracks. Think of it as having a little helper who never forgets a task. This keeps your audience engaged and moves them closer to becoming customers. You don't lift a finger after the initial setup.

## Step-by-step walkthrough: Set Up Your First Welcome Email Series in EngageBay

Let's build a simple, yet powerful, welcome email series. This workflow will automatically send a series of emails to anyone who signs up for your newsletter or a freebie. It’s perfect for your very first marketing automation setup.

### Step 1: Get Started, Find the Automation Builder

First, you need to log into your EngageBay account.
1.  Once you're in, look at the left-hand menu. You'll see a list of modules like 'CRM,' 'Marketing,' 'Sales,' and 'Service.'
2.  Click on **Marketing**.
3.  Under the Marketing section, you’ll see **Automation**. Click on that.
4.  The screen will show any existing workflows you have. Since this is your first time, it might be empty.
5.  Look for a button that says **New Automation** or **Create Automation**. It’s usually in the top right corner. Click it.

A new window will pop up. It asks you to name your automation. I always name mine clearly, like "New Subscriber Welcome Series." You can also add a description. This helps later when you have many workflows. Then, click **Create**.

### Step 2: Pick Your Trigger, The Starting Gun

Now you're in the visual workflow builder. It's a blank canvas with a single green box at the top. This green box is your **Trigger**. It tells EngageBay *when* this automation should start.
1.  Click on the green **Trigger** box.
2.  A sidebar will open on the right, showing different trigger types. You’ll see options like "Contact Created," "Form Submitted," "Email Opened," and more.
3.  For a welcome series, the most common trigger is **Form Submitted**. This means the workflow starts when someone fills out a specific form on your website.
4.  Select **Form Submitted**.
5.  Now, EngageBay asks you to choose *which* form. You need to have a form already set up in EngageBay (under Marketing > Forms). Pick the form your new subscribers use. For example, if you have a "Newsletter Signup" form, select that.
6.  You can also add conditions here. For instance, "only start if they submit form X *and* their country is Y." For our first workflow, keep it simple. Just select your form.
7.  Click **Save** at the bottom of the sidebar.

You'll see the green trigger box now shows "Form Submitted: [Your Form Name]". Great start!

### Step 3: Add Your First Email, Say Hello

After the trigger, you need to add the first action. This is usually your welcome email.
1.  You’ll see a **+** sign right below your trigger box. Click it.
2.  A new menu pops up, asking what type of action you want to add. You'll see options like "Send Email," "Add Tag," "Delay," "If/Else," etc.
3.  Select **Send Email**.
4.  The sidebar changes to let you configure your email.
    *   **Email Name:** Give it a name like "Welcome Email 1: Thanks for Subscribing!" This is for your internal reference.
    *   **Sender Name:** Your name or company name.
    *   **Sender Email:** Your email address (make sure it's verified in EngageBay).
    *   **Recipient:** This should be set to "Contact Email" by default. Don't change it.
    *   **Subject:** This is what your subscribers will see. Something like "Welcome! Here's Your First Step" or "Thanks for joining our community!"
    *   **Email Body:** This is where you write your email. Click on the **Create Email** button.
        *   The email editor will open. You can use a template or start from scratch.
        *   Write a friendly welcome message. Thank them for signing up. Tell them what to expect. Maybe offer a small gift or link to your best content.
        *   Make sure your email has a clear call to action.
        *   When you’re done, click **Save Template** or **Save and Close** (the exact button name might vary slightly).
5.  Back in the workflow sidebar, click **Save**.

Now you have your trigger and your first email connected! Look at the visual builder below. You’ll see the flow taking shape.

### Step 4: Add a Delay, Give Them Space

You don't want to bombard new subscribers with emails instantly. It's good practice to add a delay between emails.
1.  Click the **+** sign right below your first "Send Email" action.
2.  From the action menu, select **Delay**.
3.  The sidebar will ask you to set the delay time.
    *   For a welcome series, I usually start with **1 day**. This gives them time to read the first email.
    *   You can also set delays in hours or minutes.
4.  Click **Save**.

Your workflow now has: Trigger, Email 1, Delay. It’s starting to look like a real automation!

### Step 5: Add More Emails and Conditions, Build the Flow

Now, let's add a second email and look at a simple condition.
1.  Click the **+** sign below your "Delay" action.
2.  Select **Send Email** again.
3.  Configure this second email just like you did the first.
    *   **Email Name:** "Welcome Email 2: Dive Deeper"
    *   **Subject:** "Your Next Step: [Link to something useful]"
    *   Write the email body. Maybe share another piece of valuable content, a case study, or introduce another product/service.
4.  Click **Save**.

Now, let’s add a condition. What if someone already bought something after the first email? You don't want to keep sending them welcome emails.
1.  Click the **+** sign below your second email.
2.  Select **If/Else**. This creates a branching path.
3.  The sidebar will ask you to define your condition.
    *   Click **Add Condition**.
    *   You can choose from many contact properties or activities. For example, "Contact Property, Lifecycle Stage, is, Customer." Or "Deal, Status, is, Won."
    *   Let's use a simple one: "Email, Opened, Welcome Email 1: Thanks for Subscribing!" This checks if they opened the first email.
    *   You can also add "AND" or "OR" conditions.
4.  Click **Save**.

Now your workflow splits into two paths: "Yes" (they met the condition) and "No" (they didn't).
*   Under the "Yes" path, you might add another email or even end the workflow for that contact.
*   Under the "No" path, you could send a different email, maybe a re-engagement email, or a different offer.

For this first workflow, let’s keep it straightforward. If they opened the first email, we'll send them a third email. If not, we'll just end the workflow for them.
*   Click the **+** under the "Yes" branch of the If/Else.
*   Add another **Send Email** (Email 3).
*   Click the **+** under the "No" branch.
*   Select **End Automation**. This stops the workflow for that contact.

You can keep adding more emails, delays, and conditions. Build out your sequence as much as you need. A good welcome series often has 3-5 emails spread over a week or two.

### Step 6: Test and Go Live, Don't Skip This!

Before you make your automation live, you *must* test it.
1.  Look for a **Test** button, usually near the top right of the workflow builder.
2.  Click **Test**. EngageBay will ask you to select a contact from your list to run through the workflow.
3.  Choose a test contact (ideally, yourself or a colleague).
4.  The system will simulate the workflow and show you what actions would be taken for that contact. Check if the emails are being sent, delays are correct, and conditions are met.
5.  Also, send yourself a preview of each email to check for typos and formatting.

Once you’re happy with everything:
1.  Look for the **Status** toggle switch, usually near the top right. It will say "Draft" or "Inactive."
2.  Toggle it to **Active**.
3.  EngageBay will ask you to confirm. Click **Activate**.

Congratulations! Your first marketing automation workflow is now live. It will start working immediately for anyone who meets your trigger criteria.

## Frequently asked questions

### H3 Q: How do I create my very first marketing automation workflow in EngageBay?
A: You start by logging in, going to Marketing > Automation, and clicking "New Automation." Then, you pick a trigger (like "Form Submitted") and add actions like "Send Email" and "Delay" using the visual drag-and-drop builder.

### H3 Q: What are the exact steps to set up a basic email welcome series in EngageBay?
A: The steps are: 1) Go to Marketing > Automation > New Automation. 2) Set the trigger to "Form Submitted" for your signup form. 3) Add a "Send Email" action for your first welcome email. 4) Add a "Delay" (e.g., 1 day). 5) Add more "Send Email" actions for subsequent emails. 6) Test and activate.

### H3 Q: Where do I find the workflow builder in EngageBay and how do I use it?
A: You find the workflow builder under the "Marketing" section, then click "Automation." Once you create a new automation, you'll see a visual canvas where you drag and drop triggers, actions, and conditions to build your workflow.

### H3 Q: What triggers and actions should I use for my first marketing automation workflow?
A: For your first workflow, use "Form Submitted" as the trigger to start the automation. For actions, stick to "Send Email" and "Delay." You can also add simple "If/Else" conditions later to personalize the path.

### H3 Q: What are common pitfalls when setting up an automation workflow for the first time in EngageBay?
A: Common pitfalls include forgetting to test the workflow, not setting up your email sending domain correctly, making the workflow too complex too soon, or not adding enough delays between emails. Always test and start simple.
