## Migration Guide: Moving Your Business to Systeme.io (or Getting More from What You Have)

So, you're thinking about moving your whole operation. Or maybe you're already on Systeme.io and want to make sure you're using it to its full potential. This isn't just about finding an alternative. It's about making a smart move.

Here's a guide for setting up or moving to Systeme.io, keeping 2026 in mind. This applies whether you're coming from another platform or just starting fresh and want to do it right.

### 1. Plan Your Funnels and Offers
Before you touch any software, map it out. What are you selling? How will people find it? What's the journey?
*   Draw your sales funnels.
*   Decide on your lead magnet, main offer, and upsells.
*   Outline your email sequences for each step.

This planning saves you hours of fiddling later. It makes the build process much smoother.

### 2. Import Your Contacts
If you're moving from another email provider or CRM, this is step one for data.
*   **Export:** Go to your old platform. Find the "Export Contacts" option. Download a CSV file.
*   **Clean Up:** Open the CSV. Remove any old, inactive, or duplicate contacts. Systeme.io won't charge you for these. Make sure columns like "Email," "First Name," "Last Name" are clear.
*   **Import:** In Systeme.io, go to "Contacts" then "Import Contacts." Upload your CSV. Map the fields correctly.
*   **Tagging:** Assign tags to your imported contacts. For example, "Old CRM List" or "Course Buyers 2023." This helps with grouping later.

Systeme.io handles large imports well. I moved over 3,000 contacts from Mailchimp without a problem.

### 3. Recreate Your Funnels and Pages
This is where the visual building happens.
*   **Funnels Tab:** Go to "Funnels" in Systeme.io. Click "Create."
*   **Choose a Template:** Pick a template that's close to your desired look. Don't spend too much time on perfection here.
*   **Edit Pages:** Use the drag-and-drop editor. Add your text, images, and videos. Connect your forms to your email lists.
*   **Integrate Payments:** Connect your Stripe or PayPal account. Systeme.io makes this easy under "Settings" > "Payment Gateways."

I found it pretty quick to rebuild my existing funnels. The builder is simple. You won't get fancy animations, but you'll get working, good-looking pages.

### 4. Set Up Your Email Automations
Your email sequences are important for sales and nurturing.
*   **Automations Tab:** Go to "Automations" then "Workflows."
*   **Create a Workflow:** Start with a trigger, like "Contact subscribed to a form" or "Purchased a product."
*   **Add Steps:** Drag and drop email actions, delays, and logic.
*   **Write Emails:** Draft your email content directly in the editor.

I've set up welcome sequences, abandoned cart reminders, and course promotion series. They run automatically. This saves a lot of time.

### 5. Transfer Your Courses (If Applicable)
If you sell online courses, this needs careful handling.
*   **Course Creation:** Go to "Products/Sales" then "Courses." Create a new course.
*   **Module & Lecture Structure:** Recreate your course modules and lectures.
*   **Upload Content:** Upload your videos, PDFs, and text directly into each lecture.
*   **Access Rules:** Set up enrollment rules. Link it to a specific product or membership site.

I moved a 7-module course with 30+ lessons. It was mostly copy-pasting text and re-uploading videos. It took some time, but it was simple.

### 6. Test Everything Thoroughly
This is vital. Don't skip it.
*   **Test Funnels:** Go through each step of your funnel as a customer. Opt-in, go to the sales page, click "buy."
*   **Test Payments:** Use a test payment if available, or make a small actual purchase and refund it.
*   **Test Emails:** Make sure all your automated emails are sending. Check for formatting.
*   **Check Integrations:** If you have outside tools, verify they're still working.

I always run through my funnels at least three times. Once I forgot to set up the "thank you" page redirect. You learn from those mistakes!

### 7. Future-Proofing for 2026 with Systeme.io
Systeme.io is always changing. They regularly add new features.
*   **API Options:** They have an API. This means if you need something very custom, a developer can build on top of it. This offers more freedom than you might think.
*   **AI Connections:** While not fully built in yet, the platform is putting money into AI. Expect to see more AI-powered content generation for emails or page copy later on.
