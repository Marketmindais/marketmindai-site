## Step-by-step walkthrough: Setting up marketing automation in EngageBay

Here's how I set up my basic welcome series automation in EngageBay's free plan:

1.  **Create a Form:** First, I went to the "Marketing" section, then "Forms." I clicked "New Form" and chose a simple inline form.
    I added fields for Name and Email. I customized the thank-you message. Then I copied the embed code and put it on my website's "contact us" page.
2.  **Build a Landing Page (Optional):** If you don't have a website, you can use EngageBay to build a simple landing page. I did this for a specific campaign.
    You just go to "Marketing" > "Landing Pages," pick a template, and drag and drop your content. I embedded my new form on this page.
3.  **Design Your Welcome Emails:** Next, I went to "Marketing" > "Email Broadcasts" and designed three separate emails. Email 1 was a "Welcome to our community!" message.
    Email 2 offered a useful tip. Email 3 reminded them about a specific product. I saved them as templates.
4.  **Set Up Your Automation Sequence:** This is the core. I clicked "Marketing" > "Automations." Then I clicked "New Automation."
5.  **Define the Trigger:** I dragged the "Form Submission" trigger onto the canvas. I selected the specific form I created in step 1. So, when someone fills out *that* form, the automation starts.
6.  **Add Email Actions:** I dragged an "Email" action onto the canvas, connected it to the trigger, and selected my "Welcome Email 1." Then I added a "Delay" action for 2 days.
    After that, another "Email" action for "Welcome Email 2." I repeated this for "Welcome Email 3" with another delay.
7.  **Activate:** Once everything was connected and looked right, I clicked "Activate."

That's it. From then on, anyone who filled out that form automatically received my email series.
It saved me from manually sending emails. This is exactly what a small business needs to start automating lead nurturing by 2026.
