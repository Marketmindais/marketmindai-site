## Understanding Pay-Per-Lead: How to Find Verified B2B Leads Cheap Pay Per Lead

When you hear "pay-per-lead" (PPL), it means you only pay for a lead once it meets specific criteria you set. This is different from a subscription where you pay a flat fee every month, whether you use the service a lot or a little. It's also not like buying a big list all at once.

With a good B2B PPL service, you tell them exactly who you want to reach. This means defining the industry, company size, job title, location, and even technologies they use. The platform then finds leads matching those filters. You only pay when a lead is delivered that fits your needs.

But here's the catch: not all PPL services are the same. Many will just give you contact info. The "verified" part is where things get tricky. A truly verified lead means the email address is active, the LinkedIn profile is current, and the person still works at the company in that role. This is what you need to look for to avoid wasting money.
